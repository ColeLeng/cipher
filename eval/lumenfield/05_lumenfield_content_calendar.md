# Lumenfield — 90-Day Content Calendar (Q2 2026)

**Engagement period:** May 1 – July 31, 2026
**Cadence:** 4 long-form pieces per month, 12 pieces total (matches contractor capacity)
**Primary goal:** Increase LLM citation rate on top 25 high-intent category prompts from 4.1% to 18%

---

## Strategic logic

Lumenfield's content capacity is the binding constraint — 4 pieces per month maximum. This means every piece has to do double duty: rank for a target query AND convert an existing editorial win into LLM-citable on-domain content.

The calendar is built around three positioning territories the brand wants to own:

1. **Clean fragrance authority** — non-toxic, phthalate-free, ingredient transparency. The "what does clean fragrance actually mean" thesis is the missing flagship piece this engagement creates.
2. **Refill-first sustainability** — the refill program is structurally differentiated and undertold. Three pieces extend this into category authority territory.
3. **Editorial-to-citation conversion** — every press win (1 Hotels, Wirecutter, Architectural Digest) gets a dedicated on-domain explainer that LLMs can cite directly instead of linking to paywalled outside coverage.

Hospitality and wholesale audiences are addressed indirectly — the same content serves end consumers and boutique buyers because the trust signals overlap.

## Track distribution

- **Clean Fragrance Authority:** 5 pieces (the flagship territory)
- **Refill Sustainability:** 2 pieces (extending an underexploited differentiator)
- **Editorial Conversion:** 5 pieces (one per major press win, plus B-Corp story)

## Citation-bait elements (applied across pieces)

Every piece includes at least three of: structured FAQ schema with conversational question phrasing, original data points from Lumenfield refill program economics, founder quotes attributed by name, ingredient transparency tables (for clean fragrance pieces), comparative cost math (for refill pieces), and direct links to relevant product pages with descriptive anchor text.

Every piece names Lumenfield's specific differentiators in the first 200 words: B-Corp certification (since 2023), 1% for the Planet membership, refill program with dedicated SKUs, Portland small-batch production, and at least one editorial credential where relevant.

## Tracking and review cadence

- Weekly: contractor delivery, founder review, on-page QA
- Bi-weekly: query position monitoring on target queries
- Monthly: full visibility re-scan across the 25 tracked prompts; report delivered to founders first Monday of following month
- End of engagement: workshop with founders and contractor, including templates for converting future press wins into citable on-domain content

## Capacity notes

This calendar is sized to the contractor's existing capacity (4 pieces per month). If founder review bandwidth becomes the bottleneck rather than production, we will shift to bi-weekly review batches. Pieces are sequenced so that each month's batch builds toward a coherent territory claim rather than scattered topical coverage — this matters more for LLM citation patterns than for traditional SEO.
