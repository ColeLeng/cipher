# Velora — 90-Day Content Calendar (Q2 2026)

**Engagement period:** May 1 – July 31, 2026
**Cadence:** 2 long-form pieces per week, 24 pieces total
**Primary goal:** Increase LLM citation rate on top 30 high-intent category prompts from 9.2% to 25%

---

## Strategic logic

The calendar is built around three parallel content tracks that map to the diagnosed gaps:

1. **Comparison & consideration content** — fills the gap between branded search (where Velora wins) and category search (where they lose). LLMs cite this format heavily because it directly answers comparison prompts.
2. **Category authority pages** — rebuilds the thin /collections pages into editorially substantive landing experiences that can rank and be cited for non-branded category queries.
3. **Trust amplification** — extends the existing review/legitimacy moat into LLM-citable formats (review aggregations, buyer-journey case studies, transparency content).

German and French localization is treated as a parallel track in weeks 5–12. India market is deprioritized given low revenue share and resource constraints.

## Track distribution

- **Comparison & consideration:** 7 pieces
- **Category authority:** 12 pieces (including 4 localized DE/FR/ES)
- **Trust amplification:** 5 pieces

## Citation-bait elements (applied across pieces)

Every piece includes at least three of: structured comparison tables, named-entity citations (specific products, regions, price ranges), expert quotes from in-house team, original data points from Velora order patterns, FAQ schema markup with conversational question phrasing, and direct links to product pages with descriptive anchor text.

## Tracking and review cadence

- Weekly: publication confirmation, on-page QA
- Bi-weekly: query position monitoring on target queries
- Monthly: full visibility re-scan across the 30 tracked prompts; report delivered to client first Monday of following month
- End of engagement: workshop with in-house team covering brief structure, citation patterns, and ongoing query selection
